# Lecture AI – Smart Academic Intelligence

An AI-Powered Academic Study Assistant

## Project Description

Lecture AI is an AI-powered web application designed to help university students understand and revise their academic materials more efficiently.

Students can upload lecture PDFs and use artificial intelligence to generate summaries, ask questions about their documents, create practice quizzes and review their learning performance.

The goal is to bring useful AI-powered study tools together in one platform and make academic learning more organized and accessible.

## Proposed Features

1. **Student Authentication and Dashboard** – Student registration, login and a personal dashboard.
2. **PDF Upload and Document Library** – Upload, manage and access lecture PDF files.
3. **AI Lecture Summarizer** – Generate short and detailed summaries and identify important concepts.
4. **Chat with Your Lecture** – Ask questions and receive answers based on uploaded lecture materials.
5. **AI Quiz Generator** – Generate multiple-choice questions with selectable difficulty and question count.
6. **Interactive Quiz and Result History** – Complete quizzes, view scores and review previous attempts.
7. **Weak Topic Tracker** – Identify topics that need more practice based on quiz performance.
8. **Explain It Differently** – Get beginner-friendly explanations, practical examples or explanations in Bangla.

## Project Objectives

- Make lecture materials easier to understand.
- Reduce the time required to prepare study and revision materials.
- Provide AI-powered document-based assistance.
- Help students practice through automatically generated quizzes.
- Support students in identifying topics that need further revision.
